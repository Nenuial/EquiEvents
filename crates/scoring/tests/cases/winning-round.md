# Winning rounds (Siegerrunde, S17)

A winning round works like a jump-off, but the riders entitled to it are the **placed riders**
of the rounds before it (30 %, or the number set by the organiser — here `placed 4`). Whether
the points of the first round are carried into it is part of the scoring table. Riders who
withdraw, retire or are eliminated in it rank equal last of the winning round. Decided
2026-11-13 (docs/deviations.md S17).
