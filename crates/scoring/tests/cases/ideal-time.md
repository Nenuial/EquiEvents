# Ideal time (table 35, AZI)

"Barème A au chronomètre temps idéal": the show office enters the class's ideal time. Riders
rank on their penalties, least first; between equal penalties, the one closest to the ideal
time is better — over or under the same. The table has no time penalties. The maximum time is
the timekeeper's (an elimination), never the office's. Decided 2026-11-14 (docs/deviations.md
S20).
