## Tag der Jugend final: no ex aequo in ranks 1–3

final: 2, 1

2026-11-17; S38): entries tied in ranks 1–3 are separated by the sum of judges C and H's marks
for the given movements, in order — here movement 2, then movement 1. Amigo, Cassis, Bijou and
Dandy have 60 points each. Movement 2: Amigo 7.5 + 7.5 = 15, the others 14. Movement 1: Cassis
8 + 8 = 16, Bijou and Dandy 15. Bijou and Dandy are equal on both: lots are drawn, and until
the office sets their ranks by hand they are equal 3rd. Further down, ties stay: Elan and Fanny
