# Championships: jump-offs for medals (S14)

In championships every group of riders equal on score that includes a medal place has its own
jump-off: e.g. one for gold and silver among the riders equal first, one for bronze among the
riders equal on the next score. Rosson did not support this. Decided 2026-11-13
(docs/deviations.md S14). The class option is `options: medal jump-offs`.
